# Career Systems Plan

## Objective

Turn the current loop into a full career sandbox where every booking affects money, momentum, status, and future opportunities.

## Contracts

### Contract Attributes

- promotion name
- contract tier
- fights remaining
- show money
- win bonus
- finish bonus
- title bonus
- renegotiation threshold
- release risk
- short-notice expectation

### Contract Flow

1. Player receives an offer.
2. Offer quality depends on ranking, streak, hype, and marketability.
3. Accepting sets purse structure and expected activity.
4. Winning improves leverage.
5. Losing repeatedly can trigger worse offers or release.

### Future Additions

- sponsor clauses
- exclusivity clauses
- late replacement premiums
- downside guarantees for prospects

## Rankings

### Ranking Inputs

- win or loss
- method of victory
- opponent rank
- recent activity
- streak length
- short-notice context
- title-fight performance

### Ranking Rules

- quality wins move the player more than low-tier wins.
- inactivity should slowly decay position.
- champions and top contenders should be harder to displace.
- close losses to elite opponents should not punish as hard as bad losses to weak opposition.

### Title Ladder

- contender band
- eliminator band
- title challenger band
- champion state

## Rivalries

### Rivalry Triggers
